# Code of Conduct

SQID follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated, in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behavior that breaks this code, report it to the maintainers using GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, issue or discussion, or by contacting a maintainer of the [sqid-dev](https://github.com/sqid-dev) organization directly. Reports are handled privately.

Maintainers may remove comments, lock threads, or block participants who break this code.
